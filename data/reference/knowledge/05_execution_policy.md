# Execution Policy

## Best execution

The desk must take all sufficient steps to obtain the best possible result for client orders, considering price,
cost, speed, likelihood of execution and settlement, size and nature of the order. Execution quality is
monitored by comparing every parent order against arrival price and interval VWAP.

## Escalation

Deterioration of implementation shortfall or VWAP slippage of more than 5 bps against the baseline over a
one-hour window requires a written explanation. The explanation must identify the material cause(s) from the
standard catalogue: market volatility, wide spreads, low liquidity, venue degradation, technology latency,
market-data anomaly, large-order impact or unexpected price movement.

## Evidence standard

Every conclusion in an investigation report must cite evidence records: order data, execution data, market
data, order-book snapshots, system metrics, logs, documents or calculations. Numbers quoted in reports must
originate from deterministic analytics, never from model estimation.
